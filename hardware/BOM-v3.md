# Mercy Cube v3 Bill of Materials (Prototype)

- Microcontroller: ESP32-S3 or RP2040 (low-power)
- LEDs: WS2812B strip (60 LEDs)
- Vibration motor: ERM 3V
- Respiration sensor: Piezo belt or stretch sensor
- Battery: 18650 Li-ion + TP4056 charger
- Casing: 3D-printable resin (obsidian-like)
- Mycelium substrate: Oyster mushroom on coffee grounds (optional living interface)
- Connectors: Magnetic pogo pins for stacking

Total estimated cost: <$25/unit (volume)
