# Mercy Cube v3 Bill of Materials (Prototype)

- Microcontroller: ESP32-S3 or RP2040 (low-power)
- Display: Optional 1.3" OLED
- Sensors: Respiration belt or contact mic
- Vibration: ERM motor
- LEDs: WS2812B strip for holographic effects
- Casing: 3D-printable obsidian-like resin
- Power: 18650 battery + 5W solar option
- Mycelium substrate: Oyster mushroom on coffee grounds (living interface)
